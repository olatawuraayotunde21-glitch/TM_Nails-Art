About ~TM_Nails Art

Our Story & Passion

Our Story

Passion for Beauty

~TM_Nails Art was born from a deep passion for beauty and artistry. What started as a small home-based service has grown into one of Nigeria's most trusted nail studios, known for precision, creativity, and luxury.

We believe that beautiful nails are more than just aesthetics — they're a form of self-care, confidence, and personal expression. Every client who walks through our doors is treated to a premium experience designed to make them feel pampered and special.

Our team stays ahead of global trends, continuously learning new techniques and using only the highest quality products to ensure your nails are not only stunning but also healthy and long-lasting.

  • Certified nail technicians
  • Premium imported products
  • Strict hygiene protocols
  • Personalized consultations

Our Mission

To provide exceptional nail care services that combine artistry, quality, and hygiene. We aim to empower every client to express their unique beauty while maintaining the health and strength of their natural nails. Through our training programs, we also aspire to raise the standard of nail artistry across Nigeria.

Our Vision

To become Nigeria's leading destination for luxury nail artistry and professional nail education. We envision a world where every person can access premium nail care that makes them feel confident, beautiful, and valued — delivered by skilled technicians trained to international standards.
