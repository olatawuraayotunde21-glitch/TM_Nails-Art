What We Offer

Indulge in our range of premium nail services, each designed to pamper, beautify, and transform.

Manicure Services

Our manicure services go beyond a simple polish change. We provide a complete hand care experience that leaves your hands soft, your cuticles tidy, and your nails impeccably shaped and polished.

  • Classic Manicure - shaping, cuticle care & polish
  • Gel Manicure - long-lasting, chip-free shine
  • Spa Manicure - exfoliation, mask & massage
  • Nail Repair & strengthening treatments
  • Paraffin wax treatment for soft hands

Pedicure Services

Treat your feet to the luxury they deserve. Our pedicure services combine relaxation with beauty, featuring soothing soaks, expert nail care, and flawless polish application.

  • Classic Pedicure - soak, shape, cuticles & polish
  • Spa Pedicure - scrub, mask, massage & polish
  • Gel Pedicure - chip-resistant, glossy finish
  • Callus removal & foot smoothing treatment
  • Hot stone massage add-on available

Nail Art

Transform your nails into miniature masterpieces. Our nail art services range from elegant and understated to bold and extravagant. Bring your ideas or let our artists create something uniquely you.

  • Freehand nail art & custom designs
  • 3D nail art & embellishments
  • Ombre, marble & geometric patterns

  • Seasonal & occasion-themed designs
  • Chrome, holographic & cat-eye effects
